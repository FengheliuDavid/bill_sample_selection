                      One Hundred Seventh Congress

                        United States of America


                          AT THE FIRST SESSION

         Begun and held at the City of Washington on Wednesday,
             the third day of January, two thousand and one


                                 An Act


 
   To extend for 4 years, through December 31, 2005, the authority to 
    redact financial disclosure statements of judicial employees and 
                           judicial officers.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, That section 
105(b)(3)(E) of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is 
amended by striking ``2001'' each place it appears and inserting 
``2005''.

                               Speaker of the House of Representatives.

                            Vice President of the United States and    
                                               President of the Senate.
