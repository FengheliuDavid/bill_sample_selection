                                 A BILL


 
    To direct the Securities and Exchange Commission to suspend the 
               application of mark-to-market accounting.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SUSPENSION OF MARK-TO-MARKET ACCOUNTING.

    Not later than 60 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the 
Securities and Exchange Commission shall suspend, by rule, regulation, 
or order, the application of mark-to-market accounting as provided in 
Statement 157 of the Financial Accounting Standards Board for any 
reports required to be submitted to the Commission after January 1, 
2008.
