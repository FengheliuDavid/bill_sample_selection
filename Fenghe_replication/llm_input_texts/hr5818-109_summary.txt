Currency Overhaul for an Industrious Nation (COIN) Act - Amends federal law governing coins and currency to prescribe requirements for redesign and issuance of commemorative circulating $2 Federal Reserve notes. Requires selection of a new design for $2 Federal Reserve notes placed into circulation during each year of the five-year periods beginning January 1, 2007. Authorizes Federal Reserve banks to continue to place into circulation $1 Federal Reserve notes until a date determined according to certain criteria. Transfers the U.S. Mint and the Bureau of Engraving and Printing to the Federal Reserve Board. Requires the design and issuance of a circulating quarter dollar honoring the District of Columbia and each of the Territories.
