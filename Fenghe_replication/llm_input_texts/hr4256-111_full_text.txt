                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the American Recovery and Reinvestment Tax Act of 2009 to 
allow specified energy property grants to real estate investment trusts 
        without regard to the ratable share income limitations.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Sustainable Property Grants Act of 
2009''.

SEC. 2. ELIMINATION OF REIT RATABLE SHARE LIMITATION FOR ARRA GRANTS 
              WITH RESPECT TO SPECIFIED ENERGY PROPERTY.

    (a) In General.--Subsection (f) of section 1603 of the American 
Recovery and Reinvestment Tax Act of 2009 is amended by inserting after 
``Code of 1986'' the following: ``, except that subsection (d)(1) 
thereof shall not apply in the case of a real estate investment trust 
(as defined in section 856 of such Code)''.
    (b) Effective Date.--The amendment made by this section shall apply 
to grants made after the date of the enactment of this Act.
