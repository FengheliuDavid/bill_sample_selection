                                 A BILL


 
   To extend the authorization for appropriations for the Commodity 
           Futures Trading Commission for five fiscal years.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,
That this Act may be cited as the ``CFTC Reauthorization Act of 1994''.

SEC. 2. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

    Effective October 1, 1994, section 12(d) of the Commodity Exchange 
Act (7 U.S.C. 16(d)) is amended to read as follows:
    ``(d) To carry out this Act, there are authorized to be 
appropriated such sums as are necessary for each of the 1995 through 
1999 fiscal years.''.
