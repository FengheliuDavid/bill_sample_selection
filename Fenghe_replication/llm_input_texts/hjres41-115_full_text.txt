                            JOINT RESOLUTION


 
  Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of a rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange 
Commission relating to ``Disclosure of Payments by Resource Extraction 
                               Issuers''.

    Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That Congress disapproves the 
rule submitted by the


              

 Securities and Exchange Commission relating to ``Disclosure of 
Payments by Resource Extraction Issuers'' (published at 81 Fed. Reg. 
49359 (July 27, 2016)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

            Passed the House of Representatives February 1, 2017.

            Attest:

                                                                 Clerk.
