                                 A BILL


 
    To repeal subtitle B of title III of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Protection of Policyholders Act''.

SEC. 2. REPEAL OF REDOMESTICATION OF MUTUAL INSURERS PROVISION.

    (a) In General.--Subtitle B of title III of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley 
Act (15 U.S.C. 6731 et seq.) is hereby repealed.
    (b) Clerical Amendment.--The table of contents of the Gramm-Leach-
Bliley Act is amended by striking the items relating to subtitle B of 
title III.
