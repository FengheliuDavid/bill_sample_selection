                                 A BILL


 
 To amend the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 to give the 
  Special Inspector General oversight over the Small Business Lending 
                                 Fund.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Small Business Lending Fund 
Accountability Act of 2011''.

SEC. 2. TARP SPECIAL INSPECTOR GENERAL OVERSIGHT.

    Section 121(c)(1) of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 
2008 (12 U.S.C. 5231(c)(1)) is amended--
            (1) by striking ``section 101, and'' and inserting 
        ``section 101,''; and
            (2) by inserting ``and activities under section 4103, 4104, 
        or 4105 of the Small Business Jobs Act of 2010 (Public Law 111-
        240),'' before ``including''.
